Notes

Menu items close the menu declaratively (popovertargetaction="hide") — an item is a button doing its job plus one attribute. Tab/Esc/light-dismiss are native; arrow-key roving is a future enhancement module. Tooltips are CSS-only (data-tip) and visual — pair with aria-label or visible text. Where anchor positioning is missing, popovers fall back to the UA's centered position.
