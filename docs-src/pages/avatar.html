Progressive fallback

Initials and direct img children are native authored content; neither needs a module. Without avatar.js, or before it loads, a Blobatar example remains a complete initials avatar. The optional module uses Blobatar's deterministic seed and contrast-safe palette utilities locally; it makes no image or identity request.

Image anatomy

A direct img child fills the avatar and crops with object-fit: cover. Its intrinsic aspect ratio does not change the avatar's square size. Use one visual source per avatar: an image or a blobatar seed.

Attributes

blobatar is the deterministic seed. Its color fills the avatar by default and its corners follow the theme's --radius-md. Boolean contained keeps the generated body inset on the quiet surface instead. Change size with style="--avatar-size: 4rem".

Accessibility

For initials or generated paint, give a standalone avatar role="img" and the person's name with aria-label. A native image carries its name in alt and needs no image role on the wrapper. When visible text alongside the avatar already identifies the person, use aria-hidden="true" on the wrapper and an empty alt on its image to avoid announcing the name twice. Generated paint is decorative; authored initials remain its semantic fallback.
